# but not a clean headline: --curriculum-max-difficulty 0.0 was SELECTED as the
# best of ~6 ceiling arms, and selecting the max over 6 arms at a 9.5-point noise
# floor inflates the winner. So 04 re-measures the chosen config on fresh seeds.
# The tuning study picks the configuration; 04 reports the number.
